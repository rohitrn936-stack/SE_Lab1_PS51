# Lab 1 – Requirements Engineering

## Problem Statement #51 – Virtual Technical Conference Platform

| Req ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall anonymize submitted manuscripts (strip author headers and metadata) before assigning them to reviewer pools for double-blind review. | High | Pass: Reviewer sees an anonymized paper. Fail: Author identity is visible to the peer reviewer. | Required to maintain the double-blind review process. | Given requirement from the problem statement. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall allow Conference Authors to submit technical manuscripts through the conference platform. | High | Pass: A valid manuscript is successfully uploaded and submitted. Fail: A valid manuscript cannot be submitted. | Authors need a mechanism to submit papers to the conference. | Supports the paper-submission process. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allow the Track Chair to assign anonymized manuscripts to appropriate reviewer pools. | High | Pass: A submitted anonymized manuscript is assigned to a reviewer pool. Fail: The manuscript cannot be assigned. | Reviewer assignment is necessary for double-blind review. | Assignment occurs after anonymization. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall generate a conflict-free multi-track presentation schedule using room-capacity and speaker-availability constraints. | High | Pass: The generated schedule contains no room or speaker conflicts and respects room capacity. Fail: Any scheduling conflict remains. | A conflict-free schedule is required for conference presentations. | Constraints must be checked before confirmation. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall provide a real-time Q&A queue that allows participants to submit and upvote questions during conference sessions. | Medium | Pass: A participant can submit a question and another participant can upvote it. Fail: A submitted question cannot be displayed or upvoted. | The Q&A queue enables real-time interaction during sessions. | Upvotes should affect question ranking. |
| NFR-001 | Nonfunctional | The multi-track schedule generator shall resolve session room capacities and speaker availability constraints in under 10 seconds. | High | Pass: Benchmarking confirms schedule generation completes in under 10 seconds under simulated peak load. Fail: Schedule generation takes 10 seconds or longer. | Fast schedule generation is required for acceptable system performance. | Given requirement from the problem statement. |
| NFR-002 | Nonfunctional | The system shall protect author identity information and prevent reviewer access to author-identifying information throughout the double-blind review process. | High | Pass: Reviewers cannot access author-identifying information or metadata. Fail: Any author identity information is exposed to a reviewer. | Protecting author identity preserves the integrity of double-blind review. | Security requirement for the review process. |
